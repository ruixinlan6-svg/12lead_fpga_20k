## Problem and evidence

- M3 Level-1 RTL arithmetic is frozen, but no model-side quantization contract or integer golden artifact exists.
- The available PTB-XL sample is intentionally small, so this run validates semantics and file flow only; it cannot establish an accuracy gate.

## Optimization

- Method: per-tensor symmetric static PTQ with INT8 weights, calibrated activation scales, sign-symmetric nearest rounding, and explicit `[-128,127]` clipping.
- Why this method: it maps directly to the tested `requantize_clip.sv` behavior and avoids backend-specific quantizer differences before hardware mapping.
- Alternatives considered: QAT and framework backend quantization. Deferred until a full FP32 baseline and board resource estimate exist.

## Frozen acceptance criteria

- Quantization contract records scales, rounding, clipping, source registry hash, and checkpoint hash.
- Golden artifact includes float input, INT8 input, FP32 logits, and fake-quantized logits.
- Any accuracy decision is provisional until the full M1 dataset and at least two seeds are available.

## Execution

- Remote run directory: private `LRX/12lead_fpga_20k_m1/runs/20260826-1745-m2-ptq-smoke`.
- Calibration: up to 16 train samples from the M1 bounded registry; validation/test: up to 16 each.

## Results

- PTQ completed on the bounded M1 registry using 16 train samples for calibration and 16 validation/test samples.
- The quantization contract and golden vector bundle were generated with signed symmetric nearest rounding (half away from zero), INT8 clipping `[-128,127]`, and per-tensor scales.
- Validation macro-AUROC: FP32 `0.3053571428571429`, fake-INT8 `0.6087202380952381`; validation macro-F1 remained `0.04444444444444444`. Test macro-AUROC: FP32 `0.6076923076923078`, fake-INT8 `0.47435897435897434`.
- The apparent metric swings are expected from the tiny/imbalanced smoke subset and are not an accuracy acceptance result. No QAT decision is made.
