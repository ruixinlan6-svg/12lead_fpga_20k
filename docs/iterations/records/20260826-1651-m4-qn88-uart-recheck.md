## Problem and evidence

- Observed problem: Earlier WMI-only preflight reported no serial port, so the board-level status path was left unverified.
- New evidence: the serial skill enumerates COM9 and COM10 as healthy FT2232 channels; the board schematic labels FPGA PIN69 `SYS_TX` → `BL616_UART_RX` and PIN70 `SYS_RX` ← `BL616_UART_TX`.
- Primary metric or failure point: determine whether either FT2232 channel carries a UART stream from the QN88 FPGA after SRAM-only programming.

## Optimization

- Method: add a minimal QN88 UART TX heartbeat at 115200 8-N-1 on PIN69, with PIN70 reserved as UART RX and existing LEDs retained; build and download SRAM only, then perform passive reads on COM9/COM10.
- Why this method: it isolates the physical UART path without touching QSPI Flash, SDRAM contents, or the ECG model; a periodic known ASCII frame makes channel mapping observable.
- Alternatives considered and why not selected: probing only the current inference bitstream cannot produce UART bytes; Flash programming is not authorized; blind TX/RX loopback could alter board state.
- Expected mechanism: FPGA PIN69 → BL616 UART RX → USB bridge → one FT2232 serial channel should emit `QN88 UART OK\r\n` periodically if the physical path is wired and the channel mapping is correct.

## Frozen acceptance criteria

- Success threshold: Gowin build/PnR succeeds; SRAM programming reports 100%; at least one of COM9/COM10 receives the exact heartbeat bytes during a 3-second passive read.
- Failure/rollback threshold: no channel receives bytes after two passive reads, or PnR/device/programming fails; leave Flash untouched and mark UART path unverified.
- Fixed test set, thresholds and measurement conditions: COM9 and COM10, 115200 baud, 8-N-1, direct open, 3-second passive capture per channel, no data sent to the board.
